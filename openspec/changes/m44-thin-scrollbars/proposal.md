# m44: thin scrollbars that show on hover

## Why
The owner (2026-11-28): scrollbars should be invisible and show only while the mouse is over the page, like Telegram, and
thinner.

## What changes
CLI-100 (new): on a computer (a mouse), every scrollbar is 6px (thin in Firefox) and clear until the mouse is over the part
that scrolls, or the keyboard is in it. A touch screen keeps the system's own overlay scrollbars. docs/UI.md.
