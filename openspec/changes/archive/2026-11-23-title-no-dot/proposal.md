# Proposal

## Why

The owner (2026-11-23): titles on the site end with a full stop. A title is not a sentence and never ends with «.».

## What Changes

- CLI-44 (new): no title, heading, label or button text ends with a full stop, in Persian or English. A test checks the landing, the guides and the titles of the texts.
- The dots are removed from the landing titles, product titles, the hero and closing lines, two section titles and the closed gate title.
- docs/UI.md: the rule.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `client`: CLI-44.

## Impact

Client texts only.
