# Proposal

## Why

The owner sent screenshots of ngrok's home page and asked for the same style on Dalan's first page: dark, a big headline with a soft colour glow, product cards with a small traffic picture, a "try it now" block with tabs per operating system and a copy button, a security checklist, a closing "your turn" call and a large footer with a "problems we solve" menu. Inspired, not copied: Dalan's own words, colours, icons and Persian first.

## What Changes

- Public pages get a dark theme of their own (app screens keep light and dark).
- Landing page, top to bottom:
  - Header with a "Problems we solve" menu (live items link to the page; Layer 2 items say "soon").
  - Hero: big headline, one part in a colour gradient, a glow behind it, "Get started".
  - Two product cards with a traffic picture: **Bot relay** (users, Dalan with its guards, your computer; the computer only calls out) and **Sites** (visitors, Dalan, your local site), marked "soon".
  - "Try it right now": tabs Linux, macOS, Windows, Docker with the real commands to get the Dalan CLI and make a bot, addresses from the settings. A note that any Telegram bot library works by changing the server address.
  - The three steps and the three DESIGN section 13 messages (kept).
  - "Security from day one": a checklist of things Dalan really does.
  - "Your turn" closing call with the glow.
  - Footer with columns: product, problems we solve, resources, and a large faded wordmark.

Out of scope: an agent for sites (Layer 2), customer logos, testimonials, pricing, new fonts (headlines use Vazirmatn in Persian and the system serif in English).

Client only. No new setting, no server change.
